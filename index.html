Set up your Spotify app (once per browser)

Jukemonster plays music through a free Spotify app of your own (needs Spotify Premium):

  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and press Create app. No Create app button? Use developer.spotify.com/dashboard/create instead.
  2. Give it any name and description. Under Redirect URIs, add and press Add.
  3. Tick Web API and Web Playback SDK, agree to the terms and press Save.
  4. Copy the app's Client ID, paste it here and press Save.

Is this safe? A Client ID isn't a password: it only names your Spotify app, and Spotify shows it to anyone during sign-in. It can't be used to sign in as you or get into your account. Jukemonster runs entirely in your browser: your sign-in stays in this browser and only talks to Spotify, never to us. The code is open if you want to check: github.com/SzymonDevGit/jukemonster.

Friends can sign in to your app too: add their Spotify email under User Management (up to 5 people).

Play on

Only this browser is listed. Some browsers can't play Spotify, so open the Spotify app on your phone (or computer), play any song for a second, then press ↻ and pick it under Play on.

Playing on a phone? The Spotify app's notification and lock screen show the song's name. Turn off Spotify's notifications before you start, or play on a computer and pick "This browser" under Play on.
