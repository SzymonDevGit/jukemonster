Privacy

Last updated 9 November 2026 (visit counting added)

The short version: Jukemonster runs in your browser and talks straight to Spotify. Nothing you do in it is sent to us, there are no ads or cookies, and visits are only counted anonymously by Cloudflare. What it remembers is kept in your own browser, and you can wipe it at any time with the button below.

Who runs Jukemonster

Jukemonster is part of szy.monster and is run by Szymon. For anything about privacy or your data, email info@cheltenhamdata.co.uk.

What Spotify shares with Jukemonster

You sign in on Spotify's own page, through a Spotify app that you (or whoever is hosting the game) made in the Spotify developer dashboard. Jukemonster asks Spotify for permission to:

  • read your playlists, so you can pick the songs to play with;
  • see and control what's playing on your devices, and play music in this browser, so it can be the jukebox;
  • see your account's country and whether it's Premium, because Spotify only plays full songs for Premium accounts;
  • only if you save a Discovery playlist: create and change playlists, to save it to your account.

It doesn't ask for your email address. All of this goes straight between your browser and Spotify. None of it reaches szy.monster. Spotify's player script is loaded from Spotify once you're signed in. See Spotify's privacy policy.

What is kept in your browser

Jukemonster uses your browser's storage (localStorage), not cookies. It never leaves your device:

  • Your Spotify app's Client ID, so you don't paste it every time.
  • Your Spotify sign-in (a key from Spotify that lets the game act for you), until you sign out.
  • Game settings: player names, chosen playlists, mode and length, and which device to play on.
  • Your best solo scores.
  • The default song list Jukemonster looked up on Spotify, so it doesn't look them all up again.
  • Discovery: which playlist to save approved songs to, and a save waiting for your permission.

These stay until you sign out (which removes the sign-in), press Forget me, or clear this site's data in your browser.

Other companies

It doesn't use cookies or store anything in your browser, doesn't follow you to other sites, and we only see totals, never individual visitors. A small script for it loads from Cloudflare (static.cloudflareinsights.com). If you'd rather not be counted, any ad or tracker blocker stops it, and the site works the same. See how Cloudflare Web Analytics works.
  • Spotify, as above.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you can ask what data we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. We don't keep anything about you on our side, so the button above is usually all you need. If you have a question, email info@cheltenhamdata.co.uk. You can also complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the ICO.
